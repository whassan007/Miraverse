Come and Say Hi!

The Miraverse is bigger than just a website. It's a place for all of us to share, play, and learn together.

I'm so excited for you to join our community! We have two special places where you can meet other friends, share your own family stories, and even play a game with me and Tita.

Ask a grown-up to help you join!

💬 Mira's Kitchen Table

(Our Official Discord Server)

This is our friendly, family-safe chat server! It's just like Tita's big kitchen table, where everyone is welcome to pull up a chair. It's the perfect place to:

  • Share your own family recipes and cooking tips.
  • Show off your fan art, stories, and Roblox creations.
  • Chat with other kids and families who love stories and culture.
  • Get news about virtual cooking events, new stories, and special announcements!

A Note for Parents:

Our Discord server is actively moderated by the Miraverse team. It is age-gated and requires parent verification for users under 16 to ensure a safe, positive, and age-appropriate environment for everyone.

🎮 Tita's Kitchen World

(Our Official Roblox Game)

Ready to play? Jump into Tita's Kitchen World on Roblox! Tita and I are getting ready for a big family dinner, and we need your help in the kitchen. In this game, you can:

  • Explore Tita's house, the courtyard, and the bustling streets of Beirut.
  • Go on quests to find the freshest ingredients from the souk (the market).
  • Play fun mini-games to chop, mix, stir, and bake.
  • Learn to make real Lebanese recipes, like Tabbouleh, Hummus, and Knafeh!

A Note for Parents:

Tita's Kitchen World is built on the Roblox platform and uses all of its built-in safety features. Our game is rated 9+ and all chat is filtered. We strongly recommend reviewing your Roblox account's parental controls to manage settings for your child.

Our Community Guidelines

To make sure everyone has fun and feels safe, we have three simple rules. By joining our Discord or playing our game, you and your family agree to follow them.

1. Be Kind

Always use respectful words. We are a community that lifts each other up. We have a zero-tolerance policy for bullying, teasing, or any kind of meanness.

2. Be Respectful

We are a global community celebrating all cultures, backgrounds, and families. Be open-minded, curious, and respectful of people who are different from you. Hate speech is never allowed.

3. Be Safe

Never share personal information. This includes your full name, home address, phone number, school, birthday, or passwords. Mira and the official Miraverse team will never ask you for this information.

Remember: If you see something that makes you feel uncomfortable, tell a parent, guardian, or a moderator right away. We are here to help!
