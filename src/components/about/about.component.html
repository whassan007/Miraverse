I'm so glad you're here! My life is split between two amazing places: Paris, France, and Beirut, Lebanon. It can be a little confusing, but it's also full of amazing stories, delicious food, and lots of big questions. Come and explore with me!

Discover My Story

Who Am I?

Hi again! My name is Mira, and I'm 9 years old. I was born in Paris, but my Maman and Papa are from Lebanon. This means I get to have two homes! I spend most of the year in Paris, but every summer, I fly to Beirut to live with my grandmother, my Tita.

Tita's house is my other world. It smells like jasmine and za'atar, and it's where I learn all about my family's stories. Sometimes I feel a little bit like a puzzle, with a French piece and a Lebanese piece. I'm learning that I'm not one or the other... I'm both. And that's pretty special.

My Two Worlds

I belong to both, even when I feel I don't quite fit in either one.

In Paris... (10 Months)

  • 🏡 My home is a modern apartment.
  • 🥐 My breakfast is a baguette with jam.
  • 🏫 I go to school and wear a uniform.
  • 🗣️ I speak French all day.
  • 🤔 I'm "the Lebanese girl" to my friends.

In Beirut... (2 Months)

  • 🌳 My home is Tita's old stone house.
  • 🧕 My breakfast is warm man'akeesh with za'atar.
  • ☀️ It's summer break! I wear light cotton dresses.
  • 🤝 I speak a mix of French, Arabic, and English.
  • 🤔 I'm "the French cousin" to my family.

Both places are home. 💙🇱🇧🇫🇷

Meet Tita

Tita is my everything in Lebanon. She's my grandmother, my teacher, my storyteller, and my best friend during the summer. Tita's house smells like olive oil, za'atar, and jasmine. She teaches me to cook, tells me about Lebanon's history, and helps me understand what it means to be Lebanese even when I live in France.

She speaks Arabic to me, even though I answer in French sometimes. She just smiles and says, "Your heart knows Arabic even if your tongue is still learning."

Tita's Favorite Things

  • Recipe: Her famous kibbeh
  • Place: Under the big fig tree in her courtyard
  • Story: How she met my grandfather (Jiddo)

Get to Know Me Better!

🍽️ My Favorite Food

Tabbouleh! But only the way Tita makes it—with LOTS of parsley. Papa says it's more like a parsley salad, but Tita says that's the authentic Lebanese way.

📍 My Favorite Place

Under the big fig tree in Tita's courtyard. It's the perfect spot for reading, listening to Tita's stories, and thinking about big questions.

❓ My Most Asked Question

"Mais pourquoi?" (But why?). I want to understand everything! Tita says asking questions is how we learn.

✨ My Dream for the Future

To open a restaurant that serves both French and Lebanese food! Croissants AND man'akeesh. A place where people from everywhere feel at home.

😂 What Makes Me Laugh

When Tita tells stories about when Papa was little and got in trouble! She makes funny voices for everyone.

😢 What Makes Me Sad

Saying goodbye to Tita at the end of summer. Every single year. And when I try really hard to speak Arabic and the words don't come out right.
