## 1. Classification of Archaeological Confidence

Every structure in this program has been audited and assigned to one of five epistemic confidence tiers:

1. **Archaeologically Documented (PHYSICAL EVIDENCE):**
   - **Temple of the Obelisks (Byblos):** Hypaethral courtyard, elevated podium, and exactly 26 upright limestone obelisks/monoliths cataloged by Maurice Dunand.
   - **Earlier L-Shaped Temple (Byblos):** Distinct 3rd-millennium BCE stone footings exposed beneath the Obelisk Temple.
   - **Ahiram Sarcophagus (Byblos):** Royal tomb monument resting on 4 recumbent carved lions bearing the 38-word 22-letter linear Phoenician inscription.
   - **Byblos Ancient Harbour:** Natural cove with submerged ashlar moles documented by geoarchaeological soundings.
   - **Punic Cothon (Carthage):** Circular military naval harbor with central Admiral's island and radial slipways surveyed by Henry Hurst.
   - **Submerged Causeway (Motya):** 1.7 km stone-paved underwater roadway crossing the Stagnone lagoon to mainland Birgi.

2. **Textually Attested (LITERARY SOURCES):**
   - **Temple of Melqart (Tyre):** Described by Herodotus (Histories II.44) with two monumental pillars of gold and smaragdus; presented with appropriate scholarly caution.
   - **Carthage Triple Defensive Walls:** Described in Appian and Polybius; treated as reported historical descriptions rather than unverified structural blueprints.

3. **Scholarly Reconstruction:**
   - **Pre-332 BCE Tyre Island Acropolis:** Reconstructed from bathymetric cores, sediment analysis, and Neo-Assyrian Balawat gate reliefs.
   - **Phoenician Gaulos Merchantman:** Hull proportions and rig derived from the Uluburun/Cape Gelidonya wrecks, reliefs of Sennacherib at Nineveh, and Marsala mortise-and-tenon data.

4. **Legend or Mythology:**
   - **Queen Elissa / Dido Foundation Narrative:** Explicitly categorized as oral tradition and founding legend without claiming literal physical proof.
   - **Empty Throne of Astarte:** Contextualized as an aniconic cultural and religious symbol, preserving child-friendly observational respect without worship requirements.

5. **Game Adaptation:**
   - **Phoenician Heritage Gateway Hub:** Explicitly designed as a central pedagogical portal forum to enable seamless transit without corrupting historical site geometry.
