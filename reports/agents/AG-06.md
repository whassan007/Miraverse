### Implementation-Ready Specification: Mount Lebanon Destination (Roblox First)

#### 1. Spatial & Environmental Architecture (`MapBuilder.luau` Expansion)
To bring Mount Lebanon to life as a destination on Roblox while preserving child safety and performance:
- **Terraced Mountain Elevation**:
  - Rather than a purely flat floor, extend `Plaza_LB` to construct a stepped mountain terrace zone (`Terrace_MountLebanon`) ascending eastward from `(160, 10, 0)` towards `(220, 26, -40)`.
  - Constructed using modular, non-colliding pale limestone retaining walls (`#E6DEC8`) and cedar-trimmed railings (`#7B4B2A`), matching canonical item colors from `LB.json`.
- **Destination Waypoints & Anchor Parts**:
  - `Terrace_GatherOrigin_LB`: Specific anchor at `(210, 22, -30)` where wild thyme plants (*shillan za'atar*) and olive trees spawn for the gathering challenge.
  - `Hearth_Saj_LB`: Stone shelter and domed saj hearth platform situated on the mid-terrace.
  - `Lookout_MountLebanon_LB`: Elevated scenic overlook platform facing the coastal sea horizon, equipped with a `BillboardGui` landmark plaque displaying the verified coordinates `[34.142, 35.715]`.
- **Original Procedural Geometry Only**:
  - Built entirely from native Roblox primitives (`Part`, `WedgePart`, `CylinderPart`) and programmatic terrain. No third-party meshes, external textures, or unlicensed toolbox models.

#### 2. Gameplay & Quest Integration
- **Gathering Mechanic Alignment**:
  - Link `apps/roblox/src/shared/Challenges/Gather.luau` spawn nodes to `Terrace_GatherOrigin_LB`, placing harvestable za'atar nodes along limestone terrace ledges rather than randomly scattered on the plaza floor.
- **Architectural Restoration**:
  - In `apps/roblox/src/shared/Challenges/Build.luau`, the mountain stone house blueprint (`RRRR`, `CCCC`, `L..L`, `LLLL`) represents the traditional Lebanese central-hall house with terracotta roof tiles (`red_tile`) and cedar roof beams (`cedar_beam`).

#### 3. Child Safety & Platform Guardrails
- Retain strict zero-free-text chat enforcement via `TextChatService` configuration.
- Interaction restricted to predefined safe canned emotes (`"Hello!"`, `"Great job!"`, `"Follow me!"`, `"Thank you!"`) via [`apps/roblox/src/server/Core/SessionManager.luau`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/apps/roblox/src/server/Core/SessionManager.luau#L13).
- Strictly session-only progress; no DataStore collection, zero telemetry, zero COPPA/GDPR-K compliance concerns.
