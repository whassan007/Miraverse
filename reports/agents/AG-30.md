### Concrete Interactive Test Plan Overview
The implementation plan defines a test harness simulating player actions, RemoteEvents, and physical part interactions across all 25 acts:

| Mechanic Archetype | Count | Automated Simulation & Validation Criteria | Negative Test Injections |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`gather`** | 5 acts (`LB-1`, `EG-1`, `IQ-1`, `KR-1`, `FR-1`) | Spawns collectible parts matching `needs`; asserts touch detection increments server ledger; confirms part deletion upon touch. | Double-touch suppression; foreign player touch isolation. |
| **`cook`** | 5 acts (`LB-1`, `EG-1`, `IQ-1`, `KR-1`, `FR-1`) | Validates ordered sequence matching `recipe.inputs` step-by-step; verifies output item granted upon completion. | Out-of-order ingredient click resets sequence to step 0 and sends error notice. |
| **`build`** | 5 acts (`LB-1`, `EG-1`, `IQ-1`, `KR-1`, `FR-1`) | Validates ghost blueprint grid; verifies each cell `(r, c)` matched against `blueprintLegend`. | Wrong tile placement rejection; clicking empty void cells (`.`). |
| **`trade`** | 5 acts (`LB-1`, `EG-1`, `IQ-1`, `KR-1`, `FR-1`) | Checks mentor NPC click; confirms debit of player items; verifies issuance of reward item. | Insufficient inventory rejection; invalid item offers. |
| **`word`** | 5 acts (`LB-1`, `EG-1`, `IQ-1`, `KR-1`, `FR-1`) | Validates sequential 3-word vocabulary matching in dialect script and romanized transliteration. | Picking words out of order prompts error and prevents advancement. |
| **Session & Stamp** | 5 country loops | Sequentially completes acts 1–5; validates issuance of cryptographic souvenir stamp matching `MV-<CC>1-<NONCE>-<CHECKSUM>`. | Re-running completed chapter rejected; rapid RemoteEvent rate limiting. |
