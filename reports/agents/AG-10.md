### 2.3 Challenge Implementations in Roblox Luau (`apps/roblox/src/shared/Challenges/`)
Modular handlers in `apps/roblox/src/shared/Challenges/` execute within the server session:

1. **`Gather.luau` (Challenge `IQ-1-gather`):**
   - Spawns 5 physical model parts around the orchard/pantry zone: 2 wheat dough baskets (`#F3E5AB`), 2 date paste jars (`#5C2C16`), and 1 cardamom mortar (`#6E8B3D`).
   - Equips each item with a server-side `Touched` or `ProximityPrompt` listener tied to the player session.
   - Verifies item counts on the server; emits feedback to client HUD upon satisfying `wheat_dough: 2`, `date_paste: 2`, `cardamom: 1`.
2. **`Cook.luau` (Challenge `IQ-1-cook`):**
   - Interactive prep table near `Mentor_IQ`.
   - Server validates strict sequential inputs:
     1. Add `wheat_dough`
     2. Fill with `date_paste`
     3. Season with ground `cardamom`
   - Once all 3 ingredients are loaded, player triggers the wooden mould stamp action to produce `kleicha`.
   - Adult safety rule: Khalo Salam / Bibi Amal operate the oven slide; player does not touch open high flame.
3. **`Build.luau` (Challenge `IQ-1-build`):**
   - 4-row × 5-column grid spawned at `BuildOrigin_IQ`:
     - Row 1: `T T T T T` (5 `turquoise_tile`)
     - Row 2: `B P P P B` (`mud_brick`, 3 `palm_wood`, `mud_brick`)
     - Row 3: `B . . . B` (2 `mud_brick`, open courtyard center)
     - Row 4: `B B . B B` (4 `mud_brick` with entrance threshold)
   - Semi-transparent ghost parts (`Transparency = 0.6`).
   - Server checks placement when player interacts with required inventory item until all 16 active cells match the legend.
4. **`Trade.luau` (Challenge `IQ-1-trade`):**
   - At Al-Mutanabbi trade stall (`Abu Zaid` / coppersmith).
   - Validates that player inventory possesses `kleicha: 1`.
   - Decrements `kleicha` and grants `brass_teapot: 1` along with celebratory visual cue.
5. **`Word.luau` (Challenge `IQ-1-word`):**
   - Presents 3 interactive vocabulary lecterns displaying Baghdadi terms:
     - `كليچة` (*kleicha* — spiced date-filled Eid cookie)
     - `بيبي` (*bibi* — grandmother)
     - `شكو ماكو` (*shaku maku* — what's up / how is everything)
   - Player pairs phonetics/meanings in sequence; server validates completion without free-text chat input.
