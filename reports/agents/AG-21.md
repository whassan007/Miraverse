## 2. Concrete Implementation Specification: Japan Basho Journey (`JP`)

### 2.1 Route Design: Real Geography vs. Literary Journey vs. Folklore

To respect repository guidelines regarding cultural accuracy, the journey strictly separates historical geography, classic literature, and regional folklore:

- **Historical & Physical Geography (Real Places):**
  - **Fukagawa (Edo / Tokyo):** Basho's hermitage hut on the Sumida River, start of the journey in 1689.
  - **Nikko:** Sugi cryptomeria avenues, mountain foothills.
  - **Hiraizumi (Tohoku):** Historic northern valley of the Fujiwara clan, Koromogawa, and Takadachi.
  - **Matsushima (Miyagi):** Pine-covered archipelago bay.
  - **Ogaki (Gifu):** Final destination of the *Oku no Hosomichi* travel route.
  - *Globe Coordinate Pin Candidate:* N35.6895°, E139.6917° (Tokyo / Fukagawa) or N38.3719°, E141.0664° (Matsushima).
- **Literary Tradition (Classical Japanese Literature):**
  - Matsuo Basho's travel diary *Oku no Hosomichi* (奥の細道, 1689–1694).
  - Haiku poetry structure: 5-7-5 seasonal awareness (*kigo*), nature observation, walking rhythm.
- **Folklore & Folk Tradition:**
  - Traditional travel protections: woven straw sandals (*waraji*), sedge hats (*sugegasa*), traveler's tea stops (*chaya*), wayside Jizo stone figures.
