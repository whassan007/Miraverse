# CLAUDE-02 report: Roblox/Minecraft contracts and Minecraft audit of the 66 chapters

This wave was read-only. I edited no repository files and ran no deploys or Studio sessions. The only file I wrote is the plan note at `~/.claude/plans/you-are-worker-claude-02-lucky-pretzel.md`, which is outside the repo. Everything runtime-related is **NOT TESTED**.

## 1. Findings

**F1. The 66-chapter canon is consistent, but only on paper.**
- `docs/minecraft-global/narrative/source-labels-registry.json` lists 11 countries × 6 chapters. A script cross-check against the tables in `docs/minecraft-global/narrative/STORY-BIBLE.md` found 0 mismatches in id, title, source label or artifact id.
- The source-label split is 34 `contemporary_tradition`, 10 `historical_context`, 9 `folklore`, 9 `literature` and 4 `fictional_gameplay`.
- The story bible gives 56 distinct "primary mechanic" strings for the 66 chapters, so they are free text. The runtime cannot dispatch on them.
- Only the five `fictional_gameplay` rows are original game inventions: LB05, IQ06, DE03, MA04 and one more in the same family (the grep also showed those four).

**F2. Minecraft runtime covers 5 of 66 chapters.**
- The 5 countries are LB, EG, IQ, KR and FR, each with chapter 1 only.
- DE, CN, JP, RU, UA and MA have no world, structure, NPC or script. This matches `reports/minecraft-global/BASELINE.md`, which marks them `MISSING`.
- `apps/minecraft/scripts/src/contracts/index.ts` hard-codes `COUNTRIES_V10` to the 5 countries and `CHALLENGE_ORDER_V10` to gather, trade, cook, build, word.
- `PlayerQuestRecordV10` has no chapter dimension. It is keyed by country only (`quests[country]`).
- `apps/minecraft/scripts/src/state/index.ts` hard-codes `restorationStagesByCountry` to the 5 country codes. It does not read `COUNTRIES_V10`.
- `apps/minecraft/scripts/src/passport/index.ts` hard-codes `reward:<player>:<cc>:ch1` and `make(country, 1, …)`.
- `apps/minecraft/scripts/core/stamp.ts` uses a regex limited to `(LB|EG|IQ|KR|FR)`.

**F3. The stamp spec blocks chapters 2–66.**
- `packages/shared/stamp.md` §3 says chapter must be `1` and the country must be one of the 5 codes. The same restriction is in `apps/roblox/src/server/Core/Stamp.luau` (`chapter ~= "1"` → false) and in the `Stamp.make` country list.
- The registry's 11 country stamp artifacts (LB06 `lb_harbor_home_stamp`, MA06 `ma_morocco_circle_stamp` and the rest) cannot be expressed as `MV-<CC><n>-…` codes today.

**F4. Roblox is a 5-country, chapter-1-only, session-only slice.**
- `apps/roblox/src/server/Core/SessionManager.luau` takes `pack.chapters[1]` and uses a fixed `CHALLENGES_PER_CHAPTER = 5` and `VALID` for the 5 countries.
- It issues `Stamp.make(cc, 1)` once per country and holds state only in memory.
- `apps/roblox/src/server/Progression/PlayerDataStoreService.luau` and `apps/roblox/src/server/QuestEngine/ByblosQuestStateMachine.luau` are in-memory tables. They are not tied to the live path.
- `PlayerDataStoreService` has no real DataStore call, a hard-coded `restoredStages` for the 5 countries, and schema `mv.roblox.save.v6`.
- `ByblosQuestStateMachine` mints stamps as `MV-<CC>1-V06-<ts>`, which `Stamp.verify` would reject.
- `apps/roblox/src/server/challenges/ChallengeService.luau` hard-codes 5 countries.
- `apps/roblox/src/shared/Packs.luau` is generated by `tools/compiler/roblox.ts` and is not committed. `SessionManager.init()` requires it.
- `apps/roblox/src/server/Main.server.luau` only calls `MapBuilder.build()` and `SessionManager.init()`. Whether the other server modules are ever loaded is **NOT TESTED**.

**F5. Three Roblox progression stacks coexist and none are unified.**
- `SessionManager` is the live path. `ByblosQuestStateMachine` and `PlayerDataStoreService` are v0.6/v0.7 leftovers. All three are separate.
- They disagree on challenge order (`SessionManager` runs the pack's list order). They also disagree on the stamp format, token awards and event idempotency.
- The recent commit `2bd6cff` ("award chapter stamps once") touched only the live path.

**F6. The cross-platform contracts are hard-coded to the 5-country v0.4 shape.**
- `packages/contracts/index.ts` has `COUNTRIES` as a 5-tuple, a semantic ID `country:chN:quest:objective` and `SaveStateV4`, all 5-country shaped.
- `packages/content/schema.ts` has `CountryId` as a 5-member union, `Lang` with 6 values and no `de`, `zh`, `ja`, `ru`, `uk` or `ar-MA`, and `Pack`.
- `packages/contracts/v12/index.ts` and `packages/knowledge-v12/index.ts` are the home of `AgeTierV12`. I did not fully read them (see section 5).
- `packages/contracts/v14/index.ts` is a website hero contract for 5 countries. It is unrelated to gameplay.
- No file defines a shared 66-chapter catalog type. `packages/content/minecraft-global/` does not exist yet.

**F7. The existing chapter-1 content does not match the new canon.**
- `content/quests/LB.json` calls LB chapter 1 "Lebanon: Byblos Harbor & Teta Nahla's Saj".
- The registry calls LB01 "The Harbor Wakes", with artifact `lb_harbor_compass`. The legacy completion reward is a generic "Golden Keepsake".
- The same gap applies to the other four countries. The legacy mentor names (Teta Nahla, Bibi Amal, Halmeoni) are not mapped to the registry's casts.
- Some contract names also differ between layers. Examples: `npc:LB:teta_nahla` in `packages/contracts/index.ts` versus the mentor in `content/quests/LB.json`.
- The v14 hero uses "Ustadh Zayd" for Iraq and "Uncle Tariq" for Egypt, which differ from the `COUNTRY_META` mentors (Bibi Amal, Teta Samira).
- I did not resolve which names are canonical (see section 5).

**F8. Per-chapter variation is out of scope here but needed.**
- `swarm/minecraft-global/manifest.json` assigns age-band design (four tracks: 5-7, 8-10, 11-13, 14-17) to CL02 under `docs/minecraft-global/age-design/`. That is not this wave's scope, and I have not read it.
- There are existing 4-tier age cards under `content/v12/presentations/age-*` and `tests/v12/vectors/quest-age-tier-vectors.json`. I did not audit them.

**F9. Cultural-accuracy guardrails exist per country, not per chapter.**
- The registry's `culturalWarning` field is per country (e.g. DE: "Remove drowning, trauma and religious coercion from child-facing gameplay").
- No machine-checkable rule links the warnings to a chapter's allowed mechanics. Sensitive chapters need review before build: MA05, the MA Seven Saints chapter, UA04 and IQ01.
- The Minecraft chapter-1 world data is documented as `humanReviewRequired` in `NpcPlacementSpec`.

## 3. Implementation-ready specification

### 3.1 Shared catalog contract (new, platform-neutral)
Create `packages/content/minecraft-global/catalog.schema.ts` and generated `catalog.json`. This is CX01's lane.

```ts
type CountryCodeG = 'LB'|'IQ'|'EG'|'FR'|'KR'|'DE'|'CN'|'JP'|'RU'|'UA'|'MA';
type ChapterId = `${CountryCodeG}0${1|2|3|4|5|6}`;
type SourceLabel = 'folklore'|'literature'|'historical_context'|'contemporary_tradition'|'fictional_gameplay';
type AgeTrack = '5-7'|'8-10'|'11-13'|'14-17';
interface ChapterRecord {
  id: ChapterId; country: CountryCodeG; ordinal: 1|2|3|4|5|6;
  titleKey: string;                 // locale key, never inline translated text
  sourceLabel: SourceLabel;         // copied from the registry
  mechanicId: string;               // enum, see below
  artifactId: string;               // from the registry
  prerequisite: ChapterId | null;   // ordinal-1 chain inside a country; ch1 = null
  reviewStatus: 'DRAFT'|'NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW'|'APPROVED';
  guardrailIds: string[];           // link to country cultural-warning rules
  variants: Partial<Record<AgeTrack, VariantRef>>; // filled by CL02 outputs
}
```
- `mechanicId` is a closed enum mapped from the 56 free-text mechanic strings. Do not invent mappings; the mapping table is a human-reviewed artifact.
- Compiler test: exactly 11 countries, 66 chapters, unique ids, unique artifact ids, every label in the five allowed values, registry and story bible equal.
- `reviewStatus` defaults to `NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW` for every chapter. Nothing sets `APPROVED` except a human.

### 3.2 Chapter-aware stamp and reward contract
- Update `packages/shared/stamp.md` to a v1 format: `MV-<CC><chapter>-<NNNN>-<CCCC>`. The `<CC>` set becomes the 11 codes and `<chapter>` becomes 1–6. Replace "chapter must be 1" with "chapter must be 1–6 for a known country".
- This keeps old `…1-…` codes valid. The checksum payload is unchanged (`SALT + CC + chapter + nonce`), so old stamps still verify.
- Mirror in `packages/shared/stamp.ts`, `apps/minecraft/scripts/core/stamp.ts` and `apps/roblox/src/server/Core/Stamp.luau`, with a shared test-vector fixture so all three agree. The Luau side is **NOT TESTED**.
- Idempotency key everywhere: `reward:<playerId>:<chapterId>`. This replaces `…:<cc>:ch1`.
- Stamps stay decorative: no entitlements, no leaderboards, no commerce. This follows `packages/shared/stamp.md` §4.

### 3.3 Minecraft contract changes
Owner is the DX/CX lanes listed in `swarm/minecraft-global/owners.json`. This is my proposal.
- New `apps/minecraft/scripts/src/contracts/index.ts` types: `CountryIdV11` (11 codes), `ChapterId`, `PlayerQuestRecordV11 = { chapterId, lifecycle, completedChallenges, … }`, keyed `quests[chapterId]`.
- Save key `mv:global:v11:player:<id>`, per BASELINE.md §1. Migration order is: read `mv:v10:player:<id>`, then fall back to `mv:stamp:<CC>`. Map each legacy country record to `<CC>01` and keep stamps. Never delete the old keys.
- Keep the five-challenge order as the default (`gather, trade, cook, build, word`), but let a chapter declare its own `challengeKinds`. Only the registry's mechanic strings tell us which chapters differ, and CX01 has to decide that.
- `restorationStagesByCountry` becomes `Record<CountryIdV11, …>` built from the country list, not a literal.
- Chapter gating: chapter *n* needs *n−1* in the same country. Countries are open from the hub. Add a non-verbal, no-dead-end path (AG19 owns accessibility).
- Pack manifests keep `@minecraft/server@1.17.0` and `@minecraft/server-ui@1.3.0`, as in `apps/minecraft/bp/manifest.json`. Add no network modules.

### 3.4 Roblox contract changes
- Replace `VALID` and `CHALLENGES_PER_CHAPTER` in `SessionManager.luau` with a lookup from a generated `Catalog.luau` (a sibling of `Packs.luau`, generated by an extension of `tools/compiler/roblox.ts`). A `Catalog.luau` that came out of that generator is **NOT TESTED**.
- Session state becomes `{ chapterId, index, completedByChapter, stampedByChapter, … }`.
- Pick one authoritative progression path. Recommendation: keep `SessionManager` as the live router, and fold the useful parts of `ByblosQuestStateMachine` (event-id idempotency, order check) into it. Retire or clearly label the rest. Human decision needed (see section 5).
- Persistence: implement the real `DataStoreService` calls in `PlayerDataStoreService` with `UpdateAsync`, a schema version, per-chapter maps and retry/budget handling. **NOT TESTED**, and it needs Studio and API Services enabled.
- Remote validation stays server-side: the client sends only a challenge action, never a completion claim. `SecurityAndPersistence.ENABLE_ROBLOX_MONETIZATION = false` stays false.
- Roblox ships fewer chapters first. `COUNTRY_META` and the v14 hero set `isFlagshipRoblox: true` for LB only. Treat LB01–06 as the Roblox flagship and gate the other countries behind the catalog's `reviewStatus`.

### 3.5 Contract tests (to add, owner CL/CX test lanes)
1. Catalog: 11 × 6 = 66, unique ids, registry equals story bible.

4. Reward idempotency: a replay of `reward:<player>:<chapter>` grants nothing twice.
