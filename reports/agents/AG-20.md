#### Core Design Elements:
- **Literary Basis:** *The Peach Blossom Spring* (《桃花源記》, Tao Yuanming, 421 CE).
- **Primary / Secondary Colors:** `#E06D75` (Peach Blossom Pink) / `#2C5E43` (Bamboo Green).
- **Mentor:** Fisherman Tao (陶渔翁 / *Lao Tao*) at moored river skiff pier.
- **Five Playable Challenges:**
  1. `CN-1-gather`: *Drifting Petals & River Stones*
  2. `CN-1-cook`: *Spring Blossom Tea & Steamed Rice Cakes*
  3. `CN-1-build`: *The Moon Gate & Bamboo Bridge*
  4. `CN-1-trade`: *Mulberry Silk & Tea Leaves*
  5. `CN-1-word`: *Poetic Harmony of Taohuayuan* (Matching 桃花, 桑竹, 怡然)
- **Roblox Luau Module:** Concrete specification provided for `apps/roblox/src/server/MapBuilder_CN.luau` generating a cobblestone plaza, winding glass stream, cave grotto arch, and procedural peach blossom trees.
