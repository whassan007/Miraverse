### Concrete Specification
- **Offline In-Experience Localization**: Embed a deterministic `LocalizationTable` module in `apps/roblox/src/shared/Localization/Table.luau` and manager in `apps/roblox/src/shared/Localization/init.luau` resolving `en` and `fr` strings via `LocalizationService.RobloxLocaleId`.
- **Bilingual Child Safety Chat**: Overhaul the `Say` / `PhraseShown` remote events to broadcast integer phrase IDs `(1..4)`. Client instances translate the ID into their respective display language (`"Hello!"` for English peers, `"Bonjour !"` for French peers), maintaining child safety while enabling bilingual co-play.
- **Dynamic Challenge Prompts**: Refactor `ctx.notify` across challenge handlers to support parameterized key formatting (e.g. `ctx.notify("challenge.cook.added", "ok", itemName)`).
