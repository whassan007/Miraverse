### Implementation-Ready Specification for Beirut

1. **Beirut Cultural & Epistemic Demarcation**:
   - **Living Cultural Tradition & Established History**: Beirut Corniche (المنارة / الروشة), Ras Beirut, traditional sesame ka'ak street carts (كعك بعصرية مع جبنة وسمّاق), triple-arched yellow sandstone mandaloun houses (Gemmayzeh/Achrafieh heritage architecture), and coastal fishing net craft.
   - **Folklore & Mythology (Segregated)**: The legend of Saint George and the Dragon at Saint George Bay must be clearly distinguished from archaeological history and labeled as folklore.
2. **Pack Extension (`packages/content/packs/LB.json`)**:
   - **Chapter 2**: `"Morning on the Beirut Corniche"` (`"صباح على كورنيش بيروت"`).
   - **Mentor**: `Ammo Walid` (`عمّو وليد`), a friendly Beiruti fisherman and ka'ak cart vendor.
   - **5 Core Challenges**:
     - `LB-2-gather`: Gather sesame dough, sumac, and paper wrappers.
     - `LB-2-cook`: Fill and press warm sesame ka'ak with triangle cheese and sumac.
     - `LB-2-build`: Restore a Beiruti yellow sandstone house facade with triple-arched mandaloun windows and wrought-iron balconies.
     - `LB-2-trade`: Trade warm ka'ak with the Raouche lighthouse watchman for a brass maritime compass.
     - `LB-2-word`: Learn Beiruti phrases (`كعك` / *ka'ak*, `الروشة` / *al-rawsheh*, `ع راسي` / *'ala rasi*).
3. **Roblox Map Sub-Zone (`MapBuilder.luau`)**:
   - Add a regional portal connecting Byblos plaza to `Plaza_LB_Beirut` at `Vector3.new(160, 10, 80)`.
   - Feature procedural sea promenade geometry, Raouche limestone sea arches, and a bicycle ka'ak cart.
