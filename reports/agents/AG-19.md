## 2. Implementation-Ready Specification: Germany Rhine / Grail Story Environment

### 2.1 Cultural & Legendary Grounding (Distinguishing Fact, Literature & Legend)
To prevent cultural misrepresentation, the environment clearly distinguishes three layers:
- **Real Geography & Heritage (Fact)**: The Upper Middle Rhine Valley (UNESCO World Heritage Site), slate vineyards, half-timbered architectural methods (*Fachwerk*), river transport, baking traditions (such as festive baked breads/stollen/pretzels).
- **Medieval Courtly Literature (Literature)**: Wolfram von Eschenbach's 13th-century Middle High German verse romance *Parzival* (the quest of Parzival / the Grail castle Munsalväsche).
- **Folklore & Mythology (Legend)**: The Lorelei legend (rock on the Rhine bank), Rhine river sirens, and symbolic guardian themes.

*Child-Friendly Adaptation*:
Mira travels to the Rhine river valley (Bacharach / St. Goarshausen area). Guided by an elder woodcarver/baker mentor ("Opa Lorenz"), Mira learns about traditional timber-framing, river navigation, and local crafts. A legendary "Grail of Hospitality" (*Der Gastfreundschafts-Kelch*) is explored as a community artifact celebrating generosity, questioning, and sharing rather than martial combat.

### 2.3 15-Scene Narrative Outline (`packages/content/stories/de.json`)
The narrative follows Mira arriving by train along the Rhine gorge, meeting elder woodcarver Opa Lorenz and peer Lukas:
1. *Die Ankunft am Rheintal* (Arrival at the Rhine gorge / viewing hillside castles).
2. *Die Werkstatt von Opa Lorenz* (Opa Lorenz's timber workshop in Bacharach).
3. *Die Legende vom Kelch* (Hearing the legend of the Grail / Parzival's lesson: learning to ask caring questions of others).
4. *Am Flussufer* (Walking the slate riverbank; gathering river clay and driftwood).
5. *Das Fachwerkhaus* (Exploring timber-framed architecture; how oak beams interlock without iron nails).
6. *Der Sauerteig und der Dinkel* (Preparing traditional spelt sourdough in the stone oven).
7. *Der Duft von Honig und Brot* (Baking the traveller's bread; sharing with neighbors).
8. *Die Loreley-Klippe* (Viewing the tall slate rock from the ferry; discussing legends vs. navigational river currents).
9. *Die Schnitzbank* (Carving the wooden chalice on the traditional drawknife bench).
10. *Der Fehler im Muster* (Mira makes an uneven cut; Lorenz explains that handmade crafts show heart, not machine precision).
11. *Der Fährbursche Lukas* (Trading with young ferryman Lukas for chisel tools).
12. *Das Schloss auf den Höhen* (Visiting the restored castle courtyard above the vineyards).
13. *Die Frage von Parzival* (Discussing the central moral: true honor is caring for the traveller who arrives weary).
14. *Das Fest am Fluss* (Evening lantern gathering by the water; serving honey bread in the carved chalice).
15. *Ein Andenken aus Eiche* (Mira receives a souvenir oak stamp mark and looks forward to returning).
