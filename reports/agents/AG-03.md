### Concrete Specification Summary

- **Dual Selection Modalities**:
  1. **Physical 3D Hub Portals**: Circular pedestals with `ProximityPrompt` (0.5s hold duration to prevent accidental triggers), themed using canonical contracts, displaying bilingual billboards (English + Native script).
  2. **Accessible 2D Travel Map**: Child-friendly modal accessible from the HUD compass button, listing destinations in mandatory order (`LB`, `IQ`, `EG`, `FR`, `KR`) with quest progress indicator.
- **Deep-Link Launch Handling**: Safe parsing of `Player:GetJoinData().LaunchData` on startup to trigger `SelectCountry` seamlessly.
- **Server Authoritative Teleportation & State**: Synchronize all selections through `MiraVerseRemotes.SelectCountry` to update session progress, validate against rate limits, and safely reposition the character.
- **Return to Hub Flow**: Standardized return portals in all country plazas pivoting players to Lobby Center `(0, 14, 0)`.
