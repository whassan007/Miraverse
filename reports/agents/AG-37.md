* **Roblox MapBuilder Content Loader**: In [MapBuilder.luau](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/apps/roblox/src/server/MapBuilder.luau#L84), the map generator looks for `ReplicatedStorage.Shared.ContentData` or `Content`, but [tools/compiler/roblox.ts](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/tools/compiler/roblox.ts#L5) outputs to `Shared.Packs`. This causes the server to fall back to hardcoded metadata where the mentor is named `"Halmeoni Sunja"`, conflicting with canonical `"Halmeoni"` in [packages/content/packs/kr.json](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/packages/content/packs/kr.json#L24) and [packages/contracts/index.ts](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/packages/contracts/index.ts#L105).
* **Font & Hangul Glyph Rendering**: [UI.luau](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/apps/roblox/src/client/UI.luau#L20) uses `Enum.Font.FredokaOne` for buttons, which lacks complete CJK glyph sets on Roblox. CJK/Hangul support requires modern font families or system fallbacks to prevent tofu/missing-character rendering.
* **Child Safety Compliance**: Chat is disabled platform-wide via `ChatWindowConfiguration.Enabled = false` and `StarterGui:SetCoreGuiEnabled(Chat, false)` ([Main.client.luau](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/apps/roblox/src/client/Main.client.luau#L10-L16)). Only the 4 predefined phrases in the phrase bar are permitted, requiring exact culturally respectful Korean equivalents.
