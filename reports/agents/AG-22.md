### 2. Concrete Implementation Specification: Russia (`RU`) / Lake Svetloyar & Kitezh

#### A. Distinction Between Geography, Literature, and Legend
* **Real Geographical Environment:** Lake Svetloyar (озеро Светлояр), a deep, near-circular hydrologic lake located in the Voskresensky District of Nizhny Novgorod Oblast, surrounded by taiga (birch, pine, and spruce forests).
* **Literary Tradition:** Rimsky-Korsakov’s opera *The Legend of the Invisible City of Kitezh and the Maiden Fevroniya* (1907) and Pavel Melnikov-Pechersky’s 19th-century ethnographic chronicles (*In the Forests*).
* **Folk Legend:** Kitezh (Китеж-град), an invisible city submerged beneath the waters or concealed by mist, said in folklore to be revealed only to the pure-hearted, whose church bells can be heard chiming across the calm lake surface.
* **Child-Safe Adaptation:** Purely focused on the peaceful cultural tradition of gathering forest botanicals, baking traditional Karelian/Northern *kalitki* rye pastries, restoring a historic carved wooden lakeshore belfry (*zvonitsa*), and trading painted Khokhloma wooden spoons by the water's edge.
