## 2. Concrete Roblox-First Specification for Basra

- **Canal District (`CanalDistrict_Basra`):** Connected to `Plaza_IQ` at `Vector3.new(480, 10, 48)`, featuring a recessed water canal flanked by date-palm wooden quays (`#8B5A2B`).
- **Procedural Balam (Riverboat):** Anchored traditional wooden boat modeled with CSG cylinders and wedges at `(480, 10.5, 48)`.
- **Architectural Motifs (Shanashil & Date Palm Grove):**
  - Procedural wooden lattice-window overhangs (*Shanashil*) along canal facade walls.
  - Palm grove area (`DateGrove_Basra`) with cylindrical trunks and wedge fronds.
- **Dedicated Anchor Parts:**
  - `Spawn_IQ_Basra` at `Vector3.new(480, 10.5, 40)`.
  - `Mentor_IQ_Basra` at `Vector3.new(462, 13.5, 36)`.
  - `BuildOrigin_IQ_Basra` at `Vector3.new(504, 10.5, 20)`.

### B. Archetypal Challenges for Basra
1. **Gather (`gather`):** *Canal & Palm Oasis Harvest* — Collect Barhi dates (`barhi_dates` x3) and marsh reeds (`marsh_reeds` x2) around the canal/grove.
2. **Cook (`cook`):** *Dibis wa Rashi Date Treat* — Ordered recipe sequence: `inputs: ["barhi_dates", "sesame_paste", "cardamom"]` -> `output: "dibis_treat"`.
3. **Build (`build`):** *Shanashil Canal House Facade* — 4x5 blueprint grid with legend: `P` (palm wood beam), `B` (mud brick), `T` (shanashil lattice).
4. **Trade (`trade`):** *Old Basra Spice & Date Souk* — Offer `dibis_treat` and `marsh_reeds` to the harbor merchant for a jar of dried lime (`noomi_basra_jar`).
5. **Word (`word`):** *Basrawi Riverine Terms* — Match 3 authentic southern terms in order:
   - `بلم` (*balam*) — traditional wooden riverboat
   - `برحي` (*barḥī*) — renowned sweet Basra date variety
   - `شناشيل` (*shanāshīl*) — iconic oriel wooden latticework balconies
