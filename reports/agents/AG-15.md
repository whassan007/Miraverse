## 2. Concrete Implementation-Ready Specification for Busan Scope

### Cultural Context & Real Places (Subject to Human Cultural Review)
- **Setting:** Jagalchi Fish Market (`자갈치시장`) and Yeongdo coastal port in Busan.
- **Narrative Archetype:** Mentor Samchon (Uncle) or Jagalchi Halmeoni introducing coastal traditions.
- **Dish / Cultural Activity:** Making Busan fishcake skewer broth (*Busan Eomuk-tang*, `부산 어묵탕`) and crafting a coastal harbor pier / traditional fishing boat shelter.

### Proposed Pack Additions (`packages/content/packs/KR.json` — Chapter 2)

#### Chapter 2 Items
*(Note: To respect the 10-item maximum per country in `tools/validate.ts:304` or require validator adjustment for multi-chapter expansion, 2 coastal items are added to the existing 8, reaching exactly 10 items):*
1. `eomuk_paste` — "Busan Fishcake Dough" (`#F0E5D0`)
2. `kelp_radish` — "Gijang Kelp & Winter Radish" (`#2D5A3F`)

*(Existing items `pine_needles`, `giwa_tile`, `pine_pillar`, and `hanji_paper` can be reused alongside new items for building and cooking).*

#### Chapter 2 Challenges (5 archetypes)
1. **Gather (`KR-2-gather`):**
   - **Title:** "Jagalchi Market Harbor Basket"
   - **Goal:** Gather Gijang dried kelp and winter radish along the harbor stall for the simmering broth.
   - **Needs:** `[{ item: "kelp_radish", count: 3 }]`
2. **Cook (`KR-2-cook`):**
   - **Title:** "Simmer Busan Eomuk Broth"
   - **Goal:** Thread fishcake dough ribbons onto bamboo skewers, fold in fresh kelp-radish broth, and simmer.
   - **Recipe Inputs:** `["kelp_radish", "eomuk_paste"]` -> **Output:** `eomuk_paste` (or seasoned eomuk skewer)
3. **Build (`KR-2-build`):**
   - **Title:** "Busan Port Harbor Lighthouse & Beacon"
   - **Goal:** Build the harbor entrance signal platform using stone pillars and signal tiles.
   - **Blueprint:**
     ```
     GGGG
     P..P
     PHHP
     ```
   - **Blueprint Legend:** `{"G": "giwa_tile", "P": "pine_pillar", "H": "hanji_paper"}`
4. **Trade (`KR-2-trade`):**
   - **Title:** "Share Warm Broth at Jagalchi"
   - **Goal:** Offer warm fishcakes to the harbor boat captain in exchange for an inlaid celadon cup.
   - **Needs:** `[{ item: "eomuk_paste", count: 1 }]`
   - **Reward:** `[{ item: "celadon_cup", count: 1 }]`
5. **Word (`KR-2-word`):**
   - **Title:** "Busan Coastal Words"
   - **Goal:** Learn three Korean words associated with Busan's coast and markets.
   - **Words:**
     1. `바다` (*bada*) — "sea / ocean"
     2. `시장` (*sijang*) — "market"
     3. `어묵` (*eomuk*) — "fishcake"
