## 2. Implementation-Ready Technical Specification

To make the Gather challenges truly "Roblox-first," responsive, safe, and aligned with MiraVerse design guidelines, future implementations should structure Gather challenges around the following specification:

### 2.1 Spawning & Spatial Layout
Instead of spawning objects in an abstract arc in empty space:
1. **Origin Anchoring**: Use `MapBuilder.getMentorSpot(country)` or `MapBuilder.getBuildOrigin(country)` from [`apps/roblox/src/server/MapBuilder.luau`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/apps/roblox/src/server/MapBuilder.luau#L470) as the plaza anchor, rather than purely relative to player camera/character (which can clip into walls or fall off platforms).
2. **Collectable Visual Presentation**:
   - Each item is an anchored, non-colliding `BasePart` (procedural mesh/primitive using item color from pack).
   - Floats with a gentle vertical sine bobbing animation (`CFrame` update or TweenService on client).
   - Displays a `BillboardGui` with:
     - The English Item Name (e.g. "Wild Za'atar Blend").
     - Progress counter (e.g. `Collected: 1/2`).
   - Includes a gentle particle emitter or highlight ring (using the pack's `secondary` color).
3. **Double Interaction Modes**:
   - **Physical Proximity/Touch**: `Touched` event filtered strictly to `ctx.player.Character`.
   - **Click/Tap Proximity**: `ProximityPrompt` (ActionText: "Collect", HoldDuration: 0, MaxActivationDistance: 10) to support mobile touch and console navigation without collision physics glitches.

### 2.2 Server-Authoritative State & Rate Limiting
1. **Zero Client Trust**: All collection counts reside in `Gather.luau` server session state.
2. **Touch Debounce**: A per-part debounce (minimum 0.2s) to prevent a player triggering multiple touched events simultaneously on a single part.
3. **Session Teardown**:
   - On `PlayerRemoving`, immediately destroy the challenge workspace folder and disconnect listeners.
   - On challenge completion, grant items to `ctx.grant()` and fire `ctx.notify(..., "ok")`.
   - Fire `ctx.progress({ items = ..., completed = true })` over `ChallengeRemotes.State`.

### 2.3 Client-Side HUD & Audio-Visual Feedback
In [`apps/roblox/src/client/`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/apps/roblox/src/client/):
- Listen to `ChallengeRemotes.State` events for `gather` challenges.
- Render a top-screen or HUD progress bar listing each needed item with a color dot, localized item title, and fractional progress (e.g., `2/2`).
- Trigger an in-game non-copyright chime/sound (or UI sparkle tween) when an item is collected.

## 6. Open Gaps & Human Cultural Review Required

In compliance with the project's child-safety and cultural authenticity posture (`AI_REVIEWED_PENDING_HUMAN` as declared in [`README.md`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/README.md#L78-L87)):

1. **Distinction of Real Places vs. Gameplay Abstractions**:
   - *Real places*: Byblos Old Souk (Lebanon), Cairo Corniche & Khan el-Khalili (Egypt), Mutanabbi Street & Tigris Bank (Iraq), Jeonju Hanok Village (South Korea), Vieux Lyon / Les Halles de Lyon (France).
   - *Gameplay abstractions*: Floating glowing spheres/baskets in an open plaza are video game tropes and do not reflect historical market or foraging practices. Native educator feedback is required on whether items should sit in realistic artisan market stalls or baskets.
2. **Item Translations & Terminology Requiring Native Speaker Sign-Off**:
   - `LB`: Check Levantine Arabic terms for mountain za'atar blends and wild oregano gathering traditions.
   - `EG`: Verify short-grain rice and brown lentil naming in Cairene colloquial Arabic market context.
   - `IQ`: Verify date paste terminology (`khastawi` dates vs general `tamur`) used by Baghdadi families for Eid kleicha preparation.
   - `KR`: Verify Korean terminology for fresh autumn pine needles (`solip` / 솔잎) used for steaming Chuseok songpyeon to ensure culinary safety and authenticity.
   - `FR`: Verify Lyon market references (Les Halles de Lyon Paul Bocuse vs traditional neighborhood boulangeries) regarding Epiphany fève placement.
3. **Child Safety Guidelines**:
   - Ensure the narrative maintains that all heat, baking (saj, oven, steamer), and knives remain operated by adult mentors (Teta Nahla, Teta Samira, Bibi Amal, Halmeoni, Mamie Claire), while youth players engage exclusively in safe gathering, mixing, and shaping.
