## 2. Concrete Implementation-Ready Specification for Aswan

Aswan is scoped as **Chapter 2 of Egypt (`EG`)** or an **Aswan Regional District Expansion (`EG-aswan`)** within the Roblox client/server runtime.

### 2.1 Cultural & Historical Anchors (Real Places vs. Legends)
* **Real Geographical & Historical Places**:
  * *Elephantine Island (Jazirat al-Fil)*: Historic Nile river island in Aswan known for its granite rocks, ancient botanical gardens, and Nubian villages (Siou and Koti).
  * *First Cataract of the Nile*: Natural granite boulder rapids where felucca navigation requires traditional rudder and sail handling.
  * *Nubian Sandstone & Pink Granite Quarries*: Source of ancient architectural stone throughout the Nile valley.
  * *Aswan Spice Souk*: Traditional spice market renowned for hibiscus (*karkadeh*), cumin, dried mint, and dates (*tmar*).
* **Distinction from Legend / Literature**:
  * *Not a mythical land*: The Nubian cultural presence in Aswan is a living, contemporary cultural community characterized by vibrant geometric house facades, adobe vaults, woven palm basketry, and Upper Egyptian hospitality.
  * *Legendary / Folklore Context*: Ancient Nile legends regarding the annual inundation originating from the caverns of Khnum at the First Cataract are strictly categorized as historical mythology, distinguished from living Nubian culture.

### 2.2 Pedagogical & Child Safety Posture
* Strictly zero free-text chat; communication uses localized scripted phrases.
* Zero external textures, external HTTP requests, or third-party assets; visual elements are constructed using Roblox native Luau procedural primitives (smooth plastic, neon, cylinder/wedge parts, procedural geometric surface decors).
* Educational focus: River hydrology, traditional botanical tea craft (*karkadeh*), sustainable adobe / sandstone architecture, and Nubian/Upper Egyptian hospitality vocabulary.

### 2.3 Proposed Content Specification: Pack Additions (`packages/content/packs/eg.json`)
The Aswan chapter introduces Chapter 2 into `eg.json` under country `EG`:

#### New Items (Zero Collision with Global Namespace)
* `hibiscus_petals` ("Sun-Dried Karkadeh Flowers", color `#8B1E3F`)
* `aswan_dates` ("Sweet Nubian Dry Dates", color `#5C2C16`)
* `clay_cup` ("Handcrafted Aswan Clay Mug", color `#B87333`)
* `karkadeh_tea` ("Warm Spiced Karkadeh Tea", color `#780624`)
* `sandstone_block` ("Aswan Golden Sandstone Block", color `#E2B678`)
* `palm_frond_thatch` ("Woven Date Palm Frond", color `#6F8A4B`)
* `nubian_woven_plate` ("Vibrant Woven Palm Plate", color `#E5A93C`)

#### Chapter 2 Quests (5 Archetypal Mechanics)
1. **`EG-2-gather` (Gather)**: *"Botanical Gardens on Elephantine"*
   * Collect: `hibiscus_petals` (count: 3), `aswan_dates` (count: 2).
2. **`EG-2-cook` (Cook)**: *"Steeped Nile Karkadeh"*
   * Recipe inputs: `hibiscus_petals`, `aswan_dates`, `clay_cup`.
   * Recipe output: `karkadeh_tea`.
3. **`EG-2-build` (Build)**: *"Nubian Vaulted River Home"*
   * Rectangular blueprint with sandstone walls and arched palm thatch canopy:
     ```
     .TTT.
     .TTT.
     SS.SS
     SSSSS
     ```
   * Blueprint Legend: `S` = `sandstone_block`, `T` = `palm_frond_thatch`.
4. **`EG-2-trade` (Trade)**: *"Aswan Riverside Bazaar"*
   * Trade: 1 `karkadeh_tea` in exchange for 1 `nubian_woven_plate`.
5. **`EG-2-word` (Word)**: *"Greetings of the South"*
   * Vocabulary words (Arabic / Upper Egyptian / Nubian cultural loanwords):
     1. `كركديه` (*karkadeh*): Hibiscus iced or warm herbal drink.
     2. `بلح` (*balah*): Sun-dried date palm fruit.
     3. `فلوكة` (*felucca*): Wooden single-sail boat native to the Aswan cataract.
