## Concrete Implementation Specification for Alexandria (Roblox-First)

### 1. Zone & Plaza Architecture
- **Location & Spawn**: When Egypt (`EG`) is selected or region routing is chosen, Alexandria forms a sub-district or dedicated zone of the Egypt plaza in Roblox.
- **Architectural & Environmental Theme**: Mediterranean coastal Corniche palette. Primary color `#1E5B88` (Mediterranean sea blue), secondary color `#D99B26` (limestone / warm sand / lantern brass), matching [`packages/content/packs/EG.json`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/packages/content/packs/EG.json#L5-L8).
- **Physical Landmarks (Distinguished Historical & Architectural Heritage)**:
  - *The Corniche Promenade*: Sea-wall bordered plaza with fishing boat moorings.
  - *Citadel of Qaitbay Promontory*: 15th-century defensive fortress built on Pharos Island (real historical fortress).
  - *Bibliotheca Alexandrina / Ancient Library Homage*: Modern disc/reading-room geometry celebrating Alexandria as a historic global center of learning (real literature and scholarship hub).
  - *Pharos (Lighthouse of Alexandria)*: Wonder of the ancient world (historical monument/legend, distinguished from modern structures).

### 2. Challenge Suite (Alexandria Chapter / Zone Variant)
Following the 5 archetypal mechanics in [`packages/content/schema.ts`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/packages/content/schema.ts#L37):
1. **`gather` (Alexandrian Fishermen's Market on the Eastern Harbour)**:
   - Collect Mediterranean short-grain rice, fresh sea salt, and cumin from harbor crates.
2. **`cook` (Alexandrian Sayadieh or Alexandrian Kebda)**:
   - *Child-safety rule*: Adult mentor operates the heat/pan; child layers seasoned rice, caramelized onions, and cumin sauce.
   - Exact input sequence enforced by [`apps/roblox/src/shared/Challenges/Cook.luau`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/apps/roblox/src/shared/Challenges/Cook.luau).
3. **`build` (Alexandrian Fishing Boat / Falouka or Citadel Pier)**:
   - Rectangular 2D/3D ghost blueprint using `acacia_plank` and `linen_sail`.
   - Validated tile-by-tile via [`apps/roblox/src/shared/Challenges/Build.luau`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/apps/roblox/src/shared/Challenges/Build.luau).
4. **`trade` (Souk el-Attarine Exchange)**:
   - Trade assembled boat model or dish with market merchant for an Alexandrian sea-glass fanous lantern.
5. **`word` (Alexandrian Dialect Terms)**:
   - Highlight Alexandrian dialect specifics (e.g. *aywah*, *bahr*, *falouka* / distinct phonetic usage noted in [`packages/content/stories/ar-EG.json`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/packages/content/stories/ar-EG.json#L75-L76)).

### 3. Server-Side Safety & Stamp Integration
- Execution must run entirely through [`apps/roblox/src/server/Core/SessionManager.luau`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-wael-Code-Miraverse/aea78164-cdca-46b5-b396-2abe6e5bab73/scratchpad/snapshot/apps/roblox/src/server/Core/SessionManager.luau) with 0.25s remote debounce.
