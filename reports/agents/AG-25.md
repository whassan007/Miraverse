### Cultural Distinctions & Open Gaps Needing Human Review

1. **Projection Orientation (Elevation Facade vs Ground Slab)**:
   - In `LB-1-build`, `EG-1-build`, `KR-1-build`, and `FR-1-build`, the matrices represent vertical structural elevations (walls, windows, roofs, sails). Confirmation is needed from game designers on whether Roblox blocks should spawn as a vertical standing facade along the X/Y plane (recommended for readability) or flat on the floor (X/Z plane).
2. **Cultural Fact & Vocabulary Review**:
   - Architectural and maritime terms (*mandaloun*, *ramleh*, *felucca*, *shanashil*, *giwa*, *hanji*, *traboule*, *pierres dorées*) are based purely on repository packs and contracts, classified under `AI_REVIEWED_PENDING_HUMAN`. Native speaker and heritage educator sign-off is required prior to public release.
3. **Asset Policy**:
   - Zero third-party meshes or external asset IDs; all geometry will be constructed purely from Roblox primitive parts (`Part`, `WedgePart`, `CylinderPart`) and procedural Luau materials.
