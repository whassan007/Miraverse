## 2. Orthographic Conventions & Variants

- **Gaf (`گ`) and Chaf (`چ`):** Used consistently for Baghdadi reflexes of `/q/` → `/g/` (`گال`, `گدام`, `گعدت`, `گوطية`, `رفگ/تسوّگ`) and `/k/` → `/tʃ/` in lexical stems (`چان`, `حچي`, `چاي`, `باچر`, `چبير`, `سمچة`, `چفوف`).
- **2nd-Person Feminine Singular Suffix (`-ich`):** Written with standard Arabic `ج` (`شلونج`, `بيج`, `إيدج`, `أصغر منج`, `دورج`, `بيبيج`, `وراج`) in accordance with common Iraqi print orthography and to maintain exact string alignment with the canonical vocabulary entry `عاشت إيدج` (`ʿāshat īdich`).
- **Kleicha (`كليجة`) & Mould (`قالب`):** Spelled `كليجة` and `قالب` throughout the story text to match the canonical story title (`قالب بيبي أمل`) and `vocab` entries (`كليجة` / `klēcha`, `قالب` / `gālib`), while reflecting spoken Baghdadi pronunciation in the transliterations.
- **Progressive Prefix (`د-` / `دا`):** Both attached `د-` before imperfective prefixes (`دتصبغ`, `دتزوقين`, `دتلمع`) and standalone `دا` before `أ-` (`دا أشوفك`) are preserved as natural Baghdadi orthographic variants.
