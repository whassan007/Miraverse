# C3 notes (ar-IQ story)

Dialect choices for G15 review:
- Iraqi letters used: گ for /g/ (گال, گوطية) and چ for /tʃ/ (چان, باچر, چاي). قالب keeps ق in spelling; vocab translit gives the spoken /g/.
- Feminine address suffix is -ج (شلونج, إيدج). Forms: آني, هسه, هواية, أكو/ماكو, ويّا, د- progressive (دتصبغ), مال.
- Game name is an invented in-story translation: "دفر الگوطية" for kick the can, linked to Iraqi ختيلان (hide and seek).
- The game's "jail" is deliberately replaced with sitting on the picnic blanket, to avoid prison framing.
- `gloss_en` is omitted; STYLE.md requires it after Wave B.
