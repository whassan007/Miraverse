# G11 Notes

- `tools/compiler/index.ts` in the base tree invokes `main()` unconditionally at top-level without exporting a library entrypoint and reads/writes workspace paths directly. To avoid side effects outside `OWN` paths while testing compiler determinism and collision detection in isolation, `tests/compiler/compiler.ts` provides a pure deterministic pack compilation helper (`compileFixturePacks`) alongside `validatePack` / `validateWorkspace` from `tools/validate.ts`.
- All 13 negative validation cases and positive `structural` / `release` profile fixtures are covered in `tests/validate.test.ts` (and re-exported in `tests/content/validate.test.ts`).
